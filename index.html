HOUSEHOLD BUDGET · ALPHA

Let's set up your budget.

This first version is meant to use your real household information. Once you enter it, the app stores it on this device and keeps it when the app is updated.

1. Add your income

Add everyone's recurring income here. You can add as many income sources as you need.

2. Add your spending categories

These are the buckets that will get weekly allowances. You can choose a color and icon for each one.

3. You're ready.

Monthly expenses, savings, and your second income can be added from the app. You can change anything later in Settings.

Your data will be stored locally. You can export a backup anytime and import it on another device or when adding the app to your Home Screen.
