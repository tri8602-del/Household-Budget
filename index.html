Welcome to Household Budget.

Choose how you want to get started.

Create a budget for a new household, restore one you already have, or join a household someone else has already created.

1. Add your income

Money coming into your household.
Add your regular paychecks and other recurring income. For biweekly pay, enter one paycheck amount and one known payday.

2. Add your spending categories

Money you plan to spend on everyday expenses.
Categories are not bills. They are spending limits for things like groceries, gas, entertainment, dining out, and household purchases.
Think of these as maximums, not money you have to spend.

Example: Put your $180 electric bill under Bills. Put a $400 monthly limit for groceries under Categories.

3. You're ready.

Monthly bills, savings, and other details can be added from the app.

How your budget works:
Income → money coming in
Bills → required expenses
Categories → spending limits for everything else

If you spend less than a category maximum, that is a win.
